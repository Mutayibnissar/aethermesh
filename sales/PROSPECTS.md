## Research before contact

For every prospect collect:
- founder/operator name
- official website
- current product
- recent launch/funding/hiring signal
- likely workflow
- existing automation
- one evidence-backed personalization point
- appropriate buyer
- public contact channel

Do not scrape or guess personal contact details. Use public business channels and comply with applicable outreach rules.
