# AetherMesh Inbound Acquisition Engine

## Objective
Generate qualified conversations without depending on mass cold email.

Core loop: Public insight -> workflow audit -> founder conversation -> paid Opportunity Map -> pilot -> production -> managed operations.

## Positioning
AetherMesh does not sell a generic chatbot or a bag of agents. It helps companies identify one expensive/repetitive workflow, redesign it with specialist agents and human approval gates, prove it in a controlled pilot, and operate it after launch.

## Primary acquisition channels
1. Founder communities
2. Build-in-public workflow teardowns
3. Founder-to-founder direct messages
4. Ecosystem partnerships
5. Public workflow audit
6. Referral loop from completed audits

## Lead magnet
### Free AI Workflow Opportunity Scan
Input: company, workflow, current process, tools, approximate volume, main bottleneck.
Output: automation candidates, human approval points, systems/data, risk classification, evaluation metrics, pilot hypothesis.
The free scan remains diagnostic. Production implementation is paid.

## Conversion ladder
| Stage | Offer | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Free Workflow Scan | Start conversation |
| 2 | AI Workflow Opportunity Map | Paid diagnosis |
| 3 | Agent Pilot | Prove outcome |
| 4 | Production Workflow | Implementation |
| 5 | Managed Agent Operations | Recurring revenue |
| 6 | Expansion | Multiple workflows |

## Weekly acquisition rhythm
Monday: workflow teardown.
Tuesday: agent architecture.
Wednesday: founder/community engagement.
Thursday: business-case breakdown.
Friday: follow-up with engaged founders.
Weekend: compile next prospect hypotheses.

## Qualification
A lead is qualified when the workflow is real and recurring, has an identifiable owner, measurable baseline pain, accessible systems/data, pilot feasibility, and defined human approval boundaries.

## Guardrails
Never claim a prospect has a problem without evidence, needs AetherMesh, or has a specific ROI before measurement. Use evidence-backed hypotheses and invite correction.
