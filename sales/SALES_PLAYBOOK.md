# AetherMesh Sales Playbook

## The offer we sell first

**AI Workflow Opportunity Map — fixed-scope diagnostic**

In 7–10 business days, AetherMesh maps one operational workflow, identifies where specialist AI agents can safely execute work, estimates economics, defines human approval points, and produces a production roadmap.

### Buyer outcome
A decision-ready map of one workflow, not a generic AI strategy deck.

### Qualification questions
1. What workflow is repeated frequently?
2. Who performs it today?
3. What systems/data are involved?
4. What is the baseline time/cost/error rate?
5. Which actions are reversible vs consequential?
6. What would a successful pilot change?
7. Who owns the workflow and budget?

### Sales rule
Do not sell “AI agents.” Sell a measurable workflow outcome.

## Pipeline

Prospect → Researched → Contacted → Replied → Discovery → Opportunity Map → Pilot → Production → Managed Pod → Expansion

## Discovery call

1. Current workflow
2. Bottleneck
3. Baseline
4. Existing automation
5. Data/tool constraints
6. Approval boundaries
7. Desired outcome
8. Pilot scope
9. Decision process

## Objection handling

**“We can build this internally.”**
Acknowledge it. Position AetherMesh as the specialist execution layer that can benchmark the workflow, build the first version, and leave the customer with a tested operating system.

**“We already use ChatGPT/Claude.”**
The offer is not a model subscription. It is workflow orchestration, specialist roles, tools, evaluation, policy, monitoring, and operating ownership.

**“We don't know the ROI.”**
That is exactly what the Opportunity Map establishes before a larger deployment.

**“AI makes mistakes.”**
Correct. Every workflow has evaluation criteria and human approval boundaries; consequential actions are gated.

## No-go criteria

Do not pursue work when:
- there is no measurable workflow
- the buyer cannot provide required access/context
- the desired result depends on impossible data
- the customer wants unrestricted autonomous actions without controls
- success cannot be evaluated
