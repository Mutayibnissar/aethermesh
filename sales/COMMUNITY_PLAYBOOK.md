# AetherMesh Community Acquisition Playbook

## Priority ecosystem
AIBoomi is a founder-led AI community with thousands of members and active founder events. Treat it as a relationship channel, not a place for spam.

Approach:
1. Join relevant discussions.
2. Publish useful workflow research.
3. Participate in founder events.
4. Offer a public workflow teardown.
5. Move interested founders into a private discovery conversation.

## Other communities
Maintain a list of AI founder communities, SaaS founder communities, startup accelerators, venture studios, product communities, engineering communities, and operator networks.

## Participation rule
80% contribution / 20% promotion.

Do:
- answer workflow questions
- publish architecture diagrams
- share benchmarks
- show experiments
- ask for critique

Do not:
- scrape members for spam
- mass-DM
- make unsupported ROI claims
- imply endorsements that do not exist
- publish confidential customer information

## Partnership offer
Offer accelerators and founder communities a free Workflow Clinic:
- 30-minute group session
- attendees submit workflows
- analyse 2–3 live examples
- provide a lightweight framework
- interested companies can request a private Opportunity Map

## Metrics
Track community conversations, qualified inbound leads, workflow submissions, discovery calls, paid diagnostics, pilots, and production conversions.
