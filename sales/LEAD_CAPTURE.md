# AetherMesh Lead Capture Specification

## Landing page
Headline: **Find the work your company should automate with AI.**

Subheadline: **AetherMesh maps one real workflow, identifies where specialist agents can help, defines human approval points, and turns the analysis into a production pilot.**

CTA: **Get a Free Workflow Scan**

## Form
Required: name, work email, company, company website, role, workflow to analyse.
Optional: monthly workflow volume, current tools, approximate time spent, biggest bottleneck.

## Opportunity evidence
Track recurring workflow, measurable volume, measurable baseline, clear owner, accessible systems, pilot feasibility, business impact, and risk.

## Routing
High evidence -> discovery.
Medium evidence -> request more workflow information.
Low evidence -> educational content / nurture.

## Event model
- workflow_scan_submitted
- workflow_scan_reviewed
- discovery_requested
- opportunity_map_started
- pilot_started
- production_started

## Dashboard
Scans/week, qualified scans/week, discovery conversion, paid diagnostic conversion, pilot conversion, production conversion, recurring revenue, and source-to-revenue attribution.
