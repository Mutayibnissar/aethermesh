# AetherMesh Go-To-Market

## Beachhead

Start with companies where a single workflow has high frequency, clear inputs/outputs, measurable economics, and a reachable buyer.

Primary buyer profiles:
- founder/CEO of a 10–200 person company
- COO / Head of Operations
- Head of Growth
- Head of Revenue
- VP/Director of Product

## Entry offer

Lead with the **AI Opportunity Map** rather than a generic AI-agency pitch. The entry product makes the problem concrete and produces a decision artifact.

## Acquisition engine

1. Publish workflow teardown reports.
2. Offer a fixed-scope opportunity map.
3. Demonstrate one production workflow per target vertical.
4. Convert successful pilots into managed pods.
5. Expand from one workflow to adjacent workflows.

## Sales sequence

Research account → identify workflow → quantify baseline → discovery → opportunity map → paid pilot → production → managed operations → expansion.

## Differentiation

AetherMesh is not positioned as a prompt-writing shop. Its differentiator is the combination of a broad specialist-agent corpus, deterministic routing, policy controls, workflow planning, evaluation, and managed operations.

## Trust motion

Every proposal should state:
- what the system can do
- what it cannot do
- which actions require approval
- what data is processed
- where credentials live
- how outputs are evaluated
- how incidents are handled
- how the customer can stop or roll back the system

## Content moat

Continuously turn anonymized workflow learnings into benchmark reports, workflow blueprints, evaluation datasets, implementation playbooks, and industry-specific agent pods.

This creates reusable IP instead of repeatedly selling custom consulting hours.
