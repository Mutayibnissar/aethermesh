# AetherMesh Delivery Playbook

## Stage 0 — Qualification

**Owner:** Revenue + Strategy

Capture:
- business objective
- current workflow
- baseline metric
- bottleneck
- systems involved
- data sensitivity
- decision authority
- budget and timeline

Reject projects where the desired outcome cannot be measured or where access/authority cannot be established.

## Stage 1 — Discovery

Research + strategy map the current process and collect evidence. Product converts it into a workflow specification. Security identifies sensitive data and action boundaries.

Output: **Workflow Opportunity Brief**.

## Stage 2 — Design

Product/design create the target experience. Strategy defines success criteria. Engineering chooses architecture and integrations. Finance models expected unit economics.

Output: **Agent System Design**.

## Stage 3 — Build

Engineering implements. Testing creates deterministic and scenario-based evaluations. Security reviews permissions, secrets, data flow, and external actions.

Output: **Release Candidate**.

## Stage 4 — Pilot

Run on controlled real workloads. Compare against baseline. Log failures, human interventions, latency, model/tool cost, and customer value.

Output: **Pilot Evidence Report**.

## Stage 5 — Production

Project-management owns rollout. Support owns operating procedures. Security confirms production controls. Strategy/product review KPI ownership.

Output: **Production Workflow**.

## Stage 6 — Managed optimization

Weekly: operational review.

Monthly: KPI + cost + evaluation review.

Quarterly: workflow redesign and model/provider review.

## Standard acceptance gates

1. Functional acceptance
2. Evaluation threshold
3. Security/privacy review
4. Human approval boundaries
5. Cost ceiling
6. Observability
7. Rollback procedure
8. Named business owner

A workflow is not live merely because it runs. It is live when it is measurable, governed, observable, and owned.
