# AetherMesh — Agent-Native Company

AetherMesh is an AI-native operations company that designs, deploys, and operates specialist-agent systems for businesses. Instead of selling isolated chatbots, AetherMesh assembles research, strategy, product, engineering, marketing, sales, finance, security, support, testing, and project-management agents into governed workflows that produce business deliverables.

**Positioning:** AI Operating Systems for companies that need more execution capacity without building a large specialist team.

**Business model:** productized implementation + recurring managed operations + reusable workflow/IP.

## What customers buy

1. **AI Opportunity Map** — discover and prioritize automatable workflows.
2. **AI Workflow Build** — design and deploy one production workflow.
3. **AI Operating Pod** — a persistent team of specialist agents operated against a business KPI.
4. **AI Transformation Program** — multi-workflow deployment across functions.
5. **Managed Agent Operations** — monitoring, evaluation, optimization, and governance.

## Initial ICP

- B2B SaaS and technology companies
- funded startups and scale-ups
- digital-first services companies
- e-commerce and D2C operators
- teams with repetitive knowledge work and measurable workflow bottlenecks

Geography is global-first; delivery can be remote.

## Core promise

> Turn a measurable business workflow into a governed, observable AI system that can research, reason, create, execute, test, and report—with humans controlling consequential actions.

AetherMesh does **not** promise headcount elimination or guaranteed financial outcomes. Every engagement starts with a measurable baseline and an explicit success metric.

## Company architecture

The upstream Agency Agents corpus is the specialist workforce. AetherMesh adds orchestration, policy, evaluation, commercial, and delivery layers around it.

- research + strategy → discover the problem and business case
- product + design → define the solution
- engineering + testing + security → build and validate
- marketing + paid-media + sales → acquire and convert demand
- project-management + support → deliver and operate
- finance → unit economics and commercial control
- specialized + domain divisions → vertical workflows
- runtime → route, plan, govern, and evaluate work

## North-star metric

**Verified Business Workflows Live (VBWL):** customer workflows operating in production with an owner, measurable KPI, policy controls, and recurring evaluation.

Supporting metrics: time-to-first-value, workflow success rate, human approval rate, cost per successful outcome, retention, expansion revenue, gross margin, and incident rate.

## Current maturity

The repository contains the complete upstream agent corpus plus the AetherMesh runtime. This company layer is the operating blueprint and productization layer; production customer deployments still require provider credentials, integrations, security review, data contracts, and environment-specific testing.
