# AetherMesh Offer Catalog

## 1. AI Opportunity Map

**Indicative price:** $2,500–$7,500 one-time

**Duration:** 1–2 weeks

**Agents:** research → strategy → product → finance → security

**Deliverables:** workflow inventory, automation opportunities, process map, ROI model, risk register, prioritized implementation roadmap.

**Success condition:** customer leaves with a ranked set of workflows and a decision-ready implementation plan.

## 2. AI Workflow Build

**Indicative price:** $7,500–$25,000 per workflow

**Agents:** strategy → product/design → engineering → testing/security → project-management

**Deliverables:** workflow specification, agent graph, tool connections, evaluation suite, deployment documentation, operating runbook.

**Success condition:** agreed workflow passes acceptance tests in the customer's environment.

## 3. AI Operating Pod

**Indicative price:** $6,000–$20,000/month

A persistent cross-functional pod selected for one business objective.

Examples:
- Growth Pod: research + marketing + paid-media + design
- Revenue Pod: research + sales + marketing + finance
- Product Pod: research + product + design + engineering + testing
- Customer Operations Pod: support + research + project-management + engineering

**Success condition:** measurable KPI movement against a jointly defined baseline, while preserving human ownership of consequential decisions.

## 4. AI Transformation Program

**Indicative price:** $30,000–$100,000+

**Duration:** 8–16 weeks

Maps multiple workflows across a function or business unit, with governance, evaluation, training, and adoption.

## 5. Managed Agent Operations

**Indicative price:** $3,000–$15,000/month after deployment

Monitoring, evaluation, workflow optimization, incident response, cost controls, model/provider changes, quarterly workflow redesign, and reporting.

## Commercial design

Pricing is indicative rather than a promise. Final price depends on workflow complexity, integrations, data sensitivity, volume, support level, and required service levels.

Sell a defined workflow, implementation outcome, operating responsibility, and evidence of performance—not hours of AI.
