# AetherMesh — Company Foundation

## Company
**AetherMesh** is an AI operations company built on a specialist-agent execution runtime.

**Positioning:** Find the work worth automating. Design the agent system. Prove it safely. Operate it.

AetherMesh starts with an open specialist-agent corpus and adds routing, workflow planning, policy controls and evaluation so the system can be packaged into repeatable business workflows.

## First customer
Start with startups and digital-first companies that already have repetitive knowledge work and a clear workflow owner.

Initial workflow families:
- Sales research and account intelligence
- Customer-support triage and knowledge operations
- Competitive/market intelligence
- Product research and release intelligence
- Internal research and reporting

## First product
### AetherMesh Workflow Pilot

A fixed-scope product that turns one repetitive business workflow into a governed AI workflow.

**Customer receives**
1. Current-state workflow map
2. Automation opportunity map
3. Agent architecture
4. Human approval policy
5. Evaluation suite
6. Working pilot
7. Measurement report
8. Production handoff plan

**Commercial promise:** a tested workflow prototype and evidence about where automation works—not a promise of headcount reduction or guaranteed ROI.

## Operating model
Research → Diagnose → Design → Build → Evaluate → Approve → Deploy → Operate → Expand

Every consequential action follows:
**Observe → Prepare → Execute → Commit**

Commit actions that can materially affect money, customers, production systems, security, or external communications require explicit human approval unless an audited customer policy permits automation.

## What AetherMesh is not
- Not a generic chatbot agency
- Not a marketplace of prompts
- Not a claim that AI can safely replace every human workflow
- Not a guarantee of business outcomes before measurement

## North-star metric
**Verified business workflows moved from manual/repetitive to measured, governed automation.**

## First 90-day objective
Get the first 3 paid Workflow Maps, convert at least 1 into a Pilot, and use the resulting workflow/evaluation artifacts to turn the most repeatable use case into a software product.
