# AetherMesh Digital Employee Organization

AetherMesh treats specialist agents as digital employees rather than a single chatbot.

## Core employees

| Employee | Function | Primary outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy Lead | Objectives and decisions | briefs, priorities, decision memos |
| Research Analyst | Evidence and intelligence | research packs, source trails, market briefs |
| Project Manager | Coordination | plans, dependencies, status, handoffs |
| Product Lead | Product decisions | requirements, experiments, launch plans |
| Engineering Lead | Technical execution | architecture, implementation plans, QA |
| Growth Operator | Demand generation | campaigns, content, experiments |
| Revenue Operator | Commercial execution | account research, qualification, CRM actions |
| Governance Lead | Control | policies, evaluations, approvals, audit records |

## Operating model

CEO/Business outcome -> Strategy Lead -> specialist employees -> Governance Lead -> human approval -> execution.

The existing AetherMesh agent corpus remains the specialist library. The runtime provides routing, policy and evaluation. This workforce layer supplies the company-facing operating model.

## Human boundary

Agents may prepare and execute reversible, low-risk work under configured policy. Consequential or irreversible actions require explicit human approval unless an audited customer policy explicitly permits them.
