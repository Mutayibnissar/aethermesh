# AetherMesh Workforce Workflow Library

## 1. Market intelligence
Research Analyst -> source verification -> Strategy Lead -> Product Lead -> Governance Lead -> executive brief

## 2. Customer support
Support specialist -> evidence retrieval -> resolution preparation -> quality evaluation -> human approval -> response

## 3. Revenue pipeline
Research Analyst -> Revenue Operator -> account qualification -> CRM preparation -> human approval -> outreach/action

## 4. Product launch
Strategy Lead -> Research Analyst -> Product Lead -> Engineering Lead -> Growth Operator -> Governance Lead -> launch approval

## 5. IT incident
Incident specialist -> context gathering -> Engineering/IT specialist -> remediation plan -> Governance Lead -> human approval -> execution -> incident report

## 6. Company research request
Strategy Lead -> Research Analyst -> relevant specialist -> synthesis -> Governance Lead -> deliverable

Each workflow should define: trigger, inputs, owner, specialist agents, dependencies, evaluation criteria, approval points, output and rollback path.
