# AetherMesh Revenue Engine

## Objective

Turn AetherMesh from a portfolio into a repeatable customer-acquisition and expansion machine.

North-star commercial metric:
**Paid workflows in production × recurring revenue per workflow.**

## What we sell first

Do not lead with eight products. Lead with one business outcome:

> **AetherMesh finds one expensive, repetitive workflow, builds a governed AI workflow around it, and proves whether it should go to production.**

### Entry offer — Workflow Diagnostic

- Fixed fee: **$1,500**
- Delivery: 7 business days
- Buyer: COO, Head of Operations, RevOps, Support, IT, Product Operations
- Output: workflow map, automation candidates, agent design, data/integration map, risk/approval policy, evaluation plan, business-case model.
- Credit: 100% of the diagnostic fee can be credited toward a qualifying pilot signed within 30 days.

### Pilot — Workflow Pilot

- Target price: **$7,500**
- Scope: one workflow, one business owner, defined systems, governed execution, evaluation dashboard.
- Success: measurable improvement against an agreed baseline.
- No guarantee of headcount reduction or ROI.

### Production

- Target: **$15,000–$35,000 implementation**
- Recurring: **$3,000–$10,000/month managed operations**
- Expansion: additional workflows/products.

## Commercial qualification

A prospect qualifies when at least four are true:

1. The workflow repeats weekly or more.
2. A named owner exists.
3. Inputs and outputs can be measured.
4. The workflow crosses multiple tools or requires knowledge retrieval.
5. The prospect already spends money or employee time on it.
6. A controlled pilot is technically feasible.
7. The buyer can approve a pilot without a six-month procurement cycle.

## Revenue math

Illustrative, not a forecast:

- 20 qualified conversations/month
- 25% → discovery = 5
- 40% → paid diagnostic = 2
- 50% → pilot = 1
- $1,500 diagnostic + $7,500 pilot = **$9,000 new revenue/month**
- If 50% of pilots reach $4,000/month managed operations, one pilot cohort adds **$2,000 MRR** on average.

The actual funnel must be measured weekly and repriced from evidence.

## Acquisition channels

1. Founder-led LinkedIn/email outreach.
2. Warm introductions from founders, accelerators and operators.
3. AI/startup communities.
4. Public workflow teardown content.
5. Partner referrals from automation consultancies and fractional COOs.
6. Open-source credibility from the AetherMesh repository.

## Rules

- Never claim a prospect has a problem without evidence.
- Never invent ROI.
- Never claim an integration exists until tested.
- Never send autonomous consequential actions without approved policy.
- Every sales claim must map to a measurable customer outcome.
