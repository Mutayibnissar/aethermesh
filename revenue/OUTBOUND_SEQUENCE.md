# AetherMesh Outbound Sequence

## Goal

Convert a researched account into a 20-minute workflow discovery call.

## Sequence

### Touch 1 — Evidence-led email

Subject: A workflow question for {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I’m building AetherMesh, an AI workflow operations platform.

I noticed {{specific public signal}}. Teams at that stage often have at least one workflow where people repeatedly move information between {{systems/process}}.

Rather than pitch a generic AI assistant, I’m offering a short workflow diagnostic: we map one process, identify where AI can safely execute work, define approval points, and produce a pilot scope.

If {{workflow hypothesis}} is relevant at {{company}}, would a 20-minute conversation be useful?

— Mutayib
AetherMesh

### Touch 2 — Value follow-up

Subject: Re: A workflow question for {{company}}

One concrete question:

**Which recurring workflow currently requires the most human coordination across multiple tools?**

If you give me the workflow name, I can tell you what I would measure before automating it.

### Touch 3 — Product proof

Subject: One workflow, one controlled pilot

AetherMesh is designed around a simple loop:

**Map → Design → Prove → Operate**

The pilot keeps consequential actions behind explicit approval gates and evaluates the workflow against an agreed baseline.

If you have one repetitive workflow worth testing, I can scope it.

### Touch 4 — Close the loop

Subject: Close the loop?

I’ll close this out for now.

If reducing manual work in {{workflow}} becomes a priority, the offer is a fixed-scope diagnostic rather than a long consulting engagement.

Best,
Mutayib

## Personalization rules

Every first touch must contain one verified public signal. Examples:
- recent funding
- new market expansion
- hiring surge
- new product launch
- support/operations expansion
- AI initiative
- public operations bottleneck

Do not manufacture personalization.
